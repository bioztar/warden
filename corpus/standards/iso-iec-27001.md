# ISO/IEC 27001:2022 — Information security, cybersecurity and privacy protection — Information security management systems — Requirements

- **Publisher:** ISO / IEC (JTC 1/SC 27). Edition 3, published October 2022.
- **Amendment:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022/Amd 1:2024 (climate action changes to clauses 4.1/4.2), published February 2024.
- **Catalogue page:** <https://www.iso.org/standard/27001>
- **Companion (guidance, also copyrighted):** ISO/IEC 27002:2022 — control implementation guidance, <https://www.iso.org/standard/75652.html>
- **Certification rules (for audit cadence):** ISO/IEC 27006-1 / ISO/IEC 17021-1 (certification-body requirements).
- **Structure warden relies on:** management-system clauses 4–10 (Harmonized Structure) + Annex A
  reference control set: 93 controls in four themes — 5 Organizational (5.1–5.37), 6 People
  (6.1–6.8), 7 Physical (7.1–7.14), 8 Technological (8.1–8.34).
- **Transition:** certificates to the 2013 edition ceased to be valid after 31 October 2025
  (IAF MD 26 three-year transition). VERIFY against IAF MD 26 if relying on it for a specific certificate.
