# ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — Information technology — Artificial intelligence — Management system

- **Publisher:** ISO / IEC (JTC 1/SC 42). Edition 1, published December 2023.
- **Catalogue page:** <https://www.iso.org/standard/42001>
- **Related (also copyrighted):** ISO/IEC 23894:2023 (AI risk management guidance),
  ISO/IEC 42005:2025 (AI system impact assessment; published 2025-05, <https://www.iso.org/standard/44545.html>),
  ISO/IEC 42006:2025 (requirements for bodies auditing and certifying AIMS; published 2025-07,
  <https://www.iso.org/standard/42006>). Both checked on the ISO catalogue on 2026-10-18;
  42001:2023 had no amendment listed on that date.
- **Structure warden relies on:** clauses 4–10 (Harmonized Structure, incl. 6.1.2 AI risk
  assessment, 6.1.3 AI risk treatment + Statement of Applicability, 6.1.4 AI system impact
  assessment, 8.2–8.4 operational counterparts); Annex A reference control objectives and
  controls in groups A.2–A.10; Annex B implementation guidance; Annex C potential
  AI-related organisational objectives and risk sources; Annex D use across domains/sectors.
- **EU AI Act relationship:** not (as of this pointer's date) a harmonised standard under the
  AI Act; CEN-CENELEC JTC 21 is drafting the harmonised set. See
  <https://github.com/bioztar/EU-AI-Act/blob/main/corpus/standards/index.md>.
