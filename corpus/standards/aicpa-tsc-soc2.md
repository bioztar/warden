# AICPA — 2017 Trust Services Criteria for Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, and Privacy (with Revised Points of Focus — 2022)

- **Publisher:** AICPA (Assurance Services Executive Committee). Criteria 2017; points of focus revised 2022.
- **Landing page:** <https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/download/2017-trust-services-criteria-with-revised-points-of-focus-2022>
- **Reporting guide:** AICPA Guide *SOC 2® Reporting on an Examination of Controls at a Service
  Organization Relevant to Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, or Privacy* (paid).
- **Attestation standards:** AICPA SSAE / AT-C sections 105 and 205 (examination engagements).
- **Description criteria:** DC section 200 (2018 Description Criteria, revised implementation guidance 2022).
- **Structure warden relies on:** Common Criteria CC1–CC9 (Security, always in scope) +
  additional categories A1 (Availability), PI1 (Processing Integrity), C1 (Confidentiality),
  P1–P8 (Privacy). Criteria IDs only; wording lives in the AICPA document.
